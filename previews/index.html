A first walkthrough

  1. Hear the example. In preview 02, select Audio Engineer and Transition 3–4. Press Play with headphones.
  2. Take over. Choose Mix yourself and tap a speaker, or hold keys 1–6. Your choices should persist. Listen to example returns to the current point in the demonstration.
  3. Shape resonance. Select Strophe V and Mix yourself. Move the piano resonance slider, then compare with the example. The direct clarinet stays audible.
  4. Explore and compare. Try the other scenes, reset and change scenes, open the listening guide, and explore Audience → Performer Perspective. Compare preview 01 with the preserved original.

Still under review: the recording-specific cue map, the final stationary passage in Transition 3–4, resonance placement and track alignment, learning content, credits and asset permissions, and checks on other browsers and devices.
