A first walkthrough

  1. Listen. Open Preview 05, put on headphones, and press Play. Watch each amber accent pulse and fade back into the blue surrounding sound. The recorded shadow plays; the clarinetist is silent in this excerpt.
  2. Explore. Switch listening positions while the same passage continues. Try the audience, clarinetist, and audio engineer positions, or open the 3D room. Whole room shows all six speakers; tap the floor to move, drag to rotate the view, and try Eye level. Reset returns you to the starting position; Back restores the main interface.
  3. Mix. Hold keys 1–6 or touch the pads to bring speakers forward. Latch keeps a speaker selected after you release. Switch between Sound timeline and Musical score without changing your mix.
  4. Compare. Return to Listen at the same moment to hear the example. Preview 02 still contains the four earlier scenes, including piano resonance.

Preview scope: Preview 05 covers Transition I–II only. Twelve inherited accent/release pairs are restored; the final accent releases at 56.929 seconds and the background continues to the end. Exact recording-to-score alignment still needs verification. Score scrolling is manual. Recording and score credits, permissions, listening checks, and testing on other browsers/devices remain release work. Composer is deferred.
